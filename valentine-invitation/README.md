# 💕 Valentine's Day Invitation 💕

A beautiful, interactive Valentine's Day invitation web application designed to sweep your loved one off their feet!

## ✨ Features

- 🎭 **Dramatic Envelope Opening** - A cinematic experience with smoke effects and sound
- 💌 **Beautiful Letter Design** - Elegant typography and love-themed decorations
- 🎆 **Stunning Visual Effects** - Floating hearts, sparkles, and particles
- 🎮 **Interactive "No" Button** - The button escapes when hovered and eventually disappears!
- 🎉 **Celebration Finale** - Fireworks and heart explosions when they say "Yes!"
- 📱 **Fully Responsive** - Works beautifully on mobile and desktop
- 🔊 **Sound Effects** - Dramatic audio to enhance the experience

## 💝 How It Works

1. **The Envelope** - Your Valentine sees a mysterious glowing envelope with the message "יש לך הודעה מיוחדת..." (You have a special message...)

2. **The Opening** - When they click to open, dramatic smoke and sparkles appear as the envelope slowly opens

3. **The Letter** - A beautifully designed letter rises with heartfelt words and the big question: "האם תסכימי להיות הוולנטיין שלי?" (Will you be my Valentine?)

4. **The Choice**:
   - **"כן" (Yes)** - Triggers a glorious celebration with fireworks, hearts, and "ידעתי שתגידי כן!" (I knew you'd say yes!)
   - **"לא" (No)** - The button runs away! After 5 attempts, it vanishes completely 😉

## 🛠️ Customization

Edit `index.html` to personalize:
- The greeting message
- The love letter text
- The year badge (currently 2026)

Edit `styles.css` to change:
- Colors (see `:root` variables)
- Animations and effects
- Typography
